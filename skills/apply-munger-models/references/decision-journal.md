# Decision record and review

Use a compact record at the end of a full decision or opportunity review. Include only information available before the outcome; do not backfill confidence after the fact.

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Decision / date | Exact choice, owner and date. |
| Alternatives | Chosen path and best feasible alternative, including waiting when viable. |
| Why | Two or three causal beliefs that determine the call, with sources or labeled assumptions. |
| Forecast | Observable outcome by a date; use probabilities only when grounded. |
| Disconfirmation | Specific observation that weakens or falsifies the thesis. |
| Action / limit | Smallest test, resources committed, downside limit and precommitted continue, pivot or stop threshold. |
| Review date | When to compare forecast with observations. |

## Reviewing a past call

1. Read the original record and separately gather outcomes and changed circumstances.
2. Compare prediction with observation before explaining the outcome. Separate process quality from luck.
3. Identify the model that clarified the decision, the model that misled and a missed interaction.
4. Record one reusable correction to future forecasting or decision process.

If no original forecast exists, reconstruct only contemporaneously available facts and label the reconstruction retrospective. Never claim to have saved, scheduled or monitored a record without performing the corresponding action.
